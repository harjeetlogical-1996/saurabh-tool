# digitograffi — Audit PDF Framework (Claude builds these with its own PDF skill)

## TWO TIERS of PDF

TIER 1 - TEASER PDF (attach to Email 1):
  A short branded PDF that proves you did real expert work. It previews BOTH
  full reports, in two parts:
    PART 1 - Website, SEO & AI Search: 3 of the DEEPEST findings (e.g. "not
      structured to be cited by ChatGPT / AI Overviews", "competitor X outranks
      you for {service}", "product pages bury the specs buyers search for").
    PART 2 - Social Media & Ads: 3 deep findings (e.g. "no social presence /
      no Reels", "no customer video content", "no Meta/Google retargeting").
  Plus a highlighted "Bonus: Custom AI tools for your business" box with ONE
  concrete, business-specific AI example (e.g. for a dealer: "an AI sales
  assistant that answers 'which {product} fits my budget?' and books a visit
  24/7"; for a clinic: "an AI receptionist that books appointments after hours").
  Show the OVERALL SCORE at the top. Give "what" + "why it costs customers" for
  each finding, NEVER the fix. Skip basics the owner already knows (no HTTPS etc).
  End with: "This is a preview. The full audit (all {N}+ issues + fixes, the
  social and ads plan, and your AI tools roadmap) is ready - I will walk you
  through it on a 15-minute call, then the reports are yours to keep."
  Build it with your own PDF skill; design is your call, keep digitograffi branding.

TIER 2 - FULL PDFs (revealed on/after the CALL, never before): the two deep
  documents below. The prospect is invested by then, so hand them over.

For every issue, "what" + "why", never the "how to fix" (that is the paid work).

Branding: digitograffi cover, "Prepared for {business}", 15+ years, footer
"Prepared for you". Keep it skimmable: scores, short bullets, plain language.

DESIGN IS YOUR CALL: this file only fixes the CONTENT/STRUCTURE. Use your own
PDF/design skill to make it look professional and on-brand (clean layout,
colors, score visuals) however you judge best. Just keep the digitograffi
branding and cover the sections below.

================================================================
TIER 2 / FULL PDF 1 — Website + SEO + AI Readiness Deep Audit
(call only - the teaser above is a preview of this)
================================================================
1. Snapshot: overall score, 3 biggest problems up top.
2. Technical SEO: speed (mobile + desktop), mobile-friendliness, HTTPS,
   indexability, crawl errors, schema/structured data.
3. On-page SEO: title + meta tags, heading structure, URL structure,
   internal linking.
4. Content review: quality, depth, what is covered vs missing.
5. AI / GEO / AEO readiness (THE DIFFERENTIATOR - no competitor offers this):
   is the content AI-citeable? Structured to appear in ChatGPT / Perplexity /
   Google AI Overviews? Clear question-answer formatting?
6. E-E-A-T / credibility: author info, trust signals, on-site reviews,
   about/contact clarity.
7. Keywords: what they rank for now, what they should, the gaps.
8. Search intent: what people actually search for this business, does the
   site answer it?
9. Competitor: who ranks that they do not, and why.
Close: "what" + "why" per issue, no "how".

================================================================
TIER 2 / FULL PDF 2 — Social Media + Ads Audit (call only)
================================================================
1. Social presence: which platforms, how active, last post date.
2. Content analysis: what they post vs what they should (photo/video/reel mix,
   frequency, hooks).
3. If weak: specific, concrete suggestions.
4. Ads opportunity: do they need Google Ads / Meta Ads / AI-search ads? Which
   platform is their customer on, and why.
Close: the growth opportunity, framed as customer impact, not jargon.

================================================================
On the CALL
================================================================
Screen-share both PDFs. Translate each technical issue into customer impact
("here is what a customer feels when this happens"). Discuss the AI tools
opportunity. Close on scope + next step. AFTER the call, hand over both PDFs.
Then send a proposal: fix -> SEO -> conversion, with upsells (social
automation, AI chatbot, app).
