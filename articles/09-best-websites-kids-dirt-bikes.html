What to look for before buying a kids dirt bike

Safety and the right size matter most for young riders. Run through this checklist before you buy.

Kids dirt bike buying checklist

  • Size by age, not just engine: the child should be able to touch the ground with both feet on the seat. 50cc suits ages 4 to 7, 70cc to 90cc ages 7 to 12, and 110cc older or larger kids.
  • Throttle limiter: look for an adjustable speed limiter so you can cap the top speed while your child learns.
  • Transmission: automatic or semi-automatic is best for beginners, no clutch to manage.
  • Safety kill switch: a tether or remote kill switch lets a parent stop the bike instantly.
  • Electric start: easier and safer for kids than kick start.
  • Safety gear budget: plan $200 to $500 for a helmet, goggles, gloves, boots, and body armor per rider.

How much should you spend on a kids dirt bike?

  • $500 to $900: 50cc starter bikes for young children (Epic Wheelz, 360 Powersports).
  • $900 to $1,800: 70cc to 110cc youth bikes for growing and confident riders (Q9, Tribal).
  • $300 to $700: electric kids bikes with speed limits (Best Buy) for the youngest or most cautious riders.
  • $200 to $500 extra: always budget for full safety gear on top of the bike.
