What to look for when buying string trimmers

String trimmers buying checklist

  • Power type: battery for small/medium yards, gas for big or tough jobs.
  • Shaft: straight shaft for reach, curved for lighter maneuvering.
  • Cutting width: wider cuts mean fewer passes on large lawns.
  • Line feed: bump, automatic, or fixed-line heads each suit different users.
  • Battery platform: match it to tools you already own to share batteries.
  • Weight & comfort: lighter tools reduce fatigue on long sessions.

How much should you spend on string trimmers?

  • $30 to $80: corded electric trimmers for small yards.
  • $80 to $200: battery trimmers for typical suburban lawns.
  • $200 to $350: powerful gas or premium battery models.
  • $350 and up: pro-grade Husqvarna and commercial trimmers.
