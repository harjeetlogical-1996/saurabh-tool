What to look for in a shop vac

Shop vac buying checklist

  • Capacity: 4 to 6 gallons for spot cleanup, 12 to 16 gallons for a workshop or big messes.
  • Peak HP and airflow: higher peak HP and CFM mean stronger suction; do not judge by HP alone.
  • Wet and dry: confirm it handles liquids, and check whether a separate filter is needed for wet pickup.
  • Corded vs cordless: corded for sustained power; cordless (Milwaukee, DeWALT) for portability on the jobsite.
  • Filters and bags: check that replacement filters and bags are easy to find (Ridgid, Shop-Vac).
  • Warranty: Ridgid NXT models offer a lifetime motor warranty, a big plus.

How much should you spend on a shop vac?

  • $60 to $110: compact and mid-size vacs (Ridgid 4-gal, Shop-Vac) for spot cleanup.
  • $100 to $160: big workshop vacs (Ridgid 12-gal, Craftsman 16-gal) for serious messes.
  • $200 to $400: premium cordless jobsite vacs (Milwaukee PACKOUT, DeWALT cordless).
