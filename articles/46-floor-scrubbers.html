What to look for when buying industrial floor scrubbers

Industrial floor scrubbers buying checklist

  • Space size: match cleaning width and tank size to your square footage.
  • Walk-behind vs ride-on: walk-behind for tight spaces, ride-on for large areas.
  • Power: battery for cordless freedom, corded for continuous small-area use.
  • Run time: ensure the battery covers your cleaning cycle.
  • Floor type: confirm the machine suits your flooring and soil level.
  • Service & parts: choose a dealer that supports the machine long-term.

How much should you spend on industrial floor scrubbers?

  • $1,500 to $4,000: compact walk-behind scrubbers.
  • $4,000 to $8,000: mid walk-behind and small ride-on machines.
  • $8,000 to $20,000: large ride-on industrial scrubbers.
  • $20,000 and up: premium and robotic commercial machines.
