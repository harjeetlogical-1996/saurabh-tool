What to look for when buying used dirt bikes

Used dirt bikes buying checklist

  • Inspect in person: check the frame, forks, and for leaks before buying.
  • Engine health: listen for knocks, check compression and smoke.
  • Service history: ask for records and how the bike was used.
  • Title & VIN: confirm a clean title and matching VIN to avoid stolen bikes.
  • Wear items: budget for tires, chain, sprockets, and brake pads.
  • Test ride: ride it if allowed, or have a mechanic check it.

How much should you spend on used dirt bikes?

  • $500 to $1,500: older or project youth and trail bikes.
  • $1,500 to $3,500: solid used trail and beginner bikes.
  • $3,500 to $6,000: newer used motocross and trail bikes.
  • $6,000 and up: late-model used race and premium bikes.
