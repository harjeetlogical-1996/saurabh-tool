What to look for when buying trailer hitches

Trailer hitches buying checklist

  • Vehicle fit: use a fit guide to match the hitch to your exact make and model.
  • Class rating: pick the right class (I to V) for your trailer weight.
  • Tongue weight: confirm the hitch handles your loaded tongue weight.
  • Receiver size: 1-1/4 inch for light loads, 2 inch for most towing.
  • Wiring: plan for a wiring harness for trailer lights.
  • Installation: bolt-on is DIY-friendly; complex fits may need a pro.

How much should you spend on trailer hitches?

  • $100 to $200: standard receiver hitches for light loads.
  • $200 to $400: heavier-duty class III/IV hitches plus wiring.
  • $400 to $700: weight-distribution and specialty hitches.
  • $700 and up: gooseneck and fifth-wheel towing systems plus install.
