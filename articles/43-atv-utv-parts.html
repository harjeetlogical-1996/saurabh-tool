What to look for when buying ATV and UTV parts

Atv and utv parts buying checklist

  • Fitment: confirm the part matches your exact make, model, and year.
  • OEM vs aftermarket: OEM for exact replacement, aftermarket for upgrades.
  • Quality: favor reputable brands for safety-critical parts.
  • Returns: check the return policy in case fitment is off.
  • Install difficulty: some upgrades need tools or pro installation.
  • Warranty: quality parts carry warranties worth keeping records of.

How much should you spend on ATV and UTV parts?

  • $20 to $100: maintenance parts, filters, and small accessories.
  • $100 to $400: windshields, lighting, and bolt-on upgrades.
  • $400 to $1,000: lift kits, axles, and performance parts.
  • $1,000 and up: major drivetrain, suspension, and full builds.
