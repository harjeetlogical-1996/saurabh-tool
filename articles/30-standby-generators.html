What to look for when buying standby generators

Standby generators buying checklist

  • Sizing: match the generator wattage to the circuits you must run.
  • Fuel: natural gas, propane, or dual-fuel based on what your home has.
  • Transfer switch: an automatic transfer switch is essential for true standby.
  • Installation: always use a certified electrician, never DIY.
  • Warranty: longer warranties protect a major investment.
  • Noise & placement: check decibel ratings and local placement codes.

How much should you spend on standby generators?

  • $2,000 to $4,000: smaller home standby units (Briggs & Stratton, small Generac).
  • $4,000 to $7,000: mainstream 18kW to 24kW whole-home units.
  • $7,000 to $10,000: large 26kW+ Generac and Kohler units.
  • $10,000 and up: premium and commercial-grade standby plus installation.
