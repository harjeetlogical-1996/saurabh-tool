What to look for when buying refurbished electric bikes

Refurbished electric bikes buying checklist

  • Battery health: ask for the battery age and remaining capacity.
  • Inspection: prefer sellers with a documented multi-point inspection.
  • Warranty: a 1-year warranty (like Upway) is a strong sign of quality.
  • Returns: a 30-day return window protects you if something is off.
  • Brand & parts: name brands make future parts and service easier.
  • Certification: certified pre-owned beats uncertified used.
