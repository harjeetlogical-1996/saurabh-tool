What to look for when buying camping coolers

Camping coolers buying checklist

  • Ice retention: premium coolers hold ice for days, budget ones for a day or two.
  • Size: match capacity to trip length and group size.
  • Hard vs soft: hard for long trips, soft for day outings and portability.
  • Weight: a loaded hard cooler is heavy; wheels help on big models.
  • Durability: rotomolded coolers resist abuse and last for years.
  • Warranty: premium brands back coolers with strong warranties.

How much should you spend on camping coolers?

  • $30 to $80: Coleman and Igloo day-trip coolers.
  • $80 to $200: mid-range and RTIC value coolers.
  • $200 to $350: premium rotomolded coolers (YETI Tundra, RTIC).
  • $350 and up: large premium and wheeled coolers.
