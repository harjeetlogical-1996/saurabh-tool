What to check before buying a used ATV or UTV

Used ATV buying checklist

  • Get the VIN: run it to check for theft, liens, and accident history before you pay.
  • Inspect in person: check the frame for cracks, look for oil leaks, test the suspension, and listen to the engine.
  • Confirm the title: no clear title means you may not be able to register it, a deal-breaker.
  • Check hours and use: low hours and recreational use beat a hard-worked farm machine.
  • Use buyer protection: prefer eBay or a dealer (RideNow) over a no-protection private sale when possible.
  • Compare prices: use ATV Trader and Autotrader to confirm the asking price is fair.

How much should you spend on a used ATV or UTV?

  • $500 to $3,000: older or high-hour quads and budget machines (Craigslist, Facebook).
  • $3,000 to $8,000: solid used name-brand ATVs and entry side-by-sides (eBay, ATV Trader).
  • $8,000 to $15,000: newer used utility and recreation UTVs.
  • $15,000+: low-hour used performance side-by-sides and certified pre-owned (RideNow).
