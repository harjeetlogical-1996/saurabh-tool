What to look for when buying robotic lawn mowers

Robotic lawn mowers buying checklist

  • Lawn size: match the mower's rated coverage to your yard's square footage.
  • Navigation: wire-free RTK/LiDAR/vision beats old boundary-wire systems.
  • Slopes: check the maximum incline if your yard is hilly.
  • Obstacles: vision and LiDAR handle trees, beds, and toys better.
  • Connectivity: app control and theft tracking add convenience and security.
  • Warranty: confirm coverage on the mower and the battery.

How much should you spend on robotic lawn mowers?

  • $600 to $1,000: value robot mowers for small lawns (Worx Vision).
  • $1,000 to $1,800: mid-range wire-free models (Segway Navimow i-series).
  • $1,800 to $3,000: large-lawn and advanced navigation models.
  • $3,000 and up: premium Husqvarna and big-acreage robot mowers.
