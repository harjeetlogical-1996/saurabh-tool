What to look for before buying a generator online

Before you click buy, run through this quick checklist. It is the same one we used to score the stores above.

Generator buying checklist

  • Power type: solar power stations are quiet and indoor-safe; gas generators make more power but must run outdoors; standby units power a whole home automatically.
  • Capacity: for power stations, look at watt-hours (Wh) for runtime and watts (W) for what it can run at once. 1000Wh covers phones, lights, and a fridge for hours.
  • Running vs starting watts: for gas generators, match starting watts to your biggest appliance surge, not just the running total.
  • Fuel: dual-fuel gas units (gas or propane) give you flexibility during shortages.
  • Warranty: 2 years is common; Anker SOLIX offers 5 years, which is a real plus on a battery.
  • Installation: whole-home standby generators require professional installation and a transfer switch. Budget for it.

How much should you spend on a generator?

Here is a realistic budget guide based on the live pricing we saw across these stores:

  • $250 to $600: small solar power stations (Anker C300, Jackery Explorer 1000). Great for camping, phones, and short outages.
  • $600 to $1,200: mid-size power stations (EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus, Bluetti AC180) that can run a fridge and essentials for hours.
  • $1,200 to $2,500: large solar systems and portable gas generators (DuroMax dual-fuel) for serious backup or a job site.
  • $5,000 and up: whole-home standby generators (Generac) with professional installation for automatic, hands-off backup.

Red flags to avoid when buying a generator online

Watch out for these

  • Power station listings that quote watt-hours but hide the continuous watt output.
  • Gas generator wattage that looks too high for the price; check starting vs running watts.
  • No clear battery or engine warranty, or a warranty that is hard to find.
  • Unknown brands far cheaper than EcoFlow, Jackery, or Anker with no real reviews.
  • Standby generators sold without mention of required professional installation.
