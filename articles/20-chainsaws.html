What to look for in a chainsaw

Chainsaw buying checklist

  • Gas vs battery: battery saws are quiet, low-maintenance, and great for the yard; gas saws cut faster and longer for firewood and felling.
  • Bar length: 14 to 16 inches for limbs and small trees; 18 to 20 inches for firewood; 20-plus inches for big trees.
  • Power: match engine cc (gas) or voltage (battery, 40V to 80V) to the size of wood you cut.
  • Safety features: look for a chain brake, low-kickback chain, and a comfortable grip.
  • Battery platform: if going battery, stick to one brand (EGO, STIHL) so batteries are shared across tools.
  • Warranty & parts: buy from a brand with dealers or easy access to chains and bars.

How much should you spend on a chainsaw?

  • $130 to $250: homeowner gas and battery saws (STIHL MS 170, EGO CS1611) for yard work and limbs.
  • $250 to $450: mid-size gas and premium battery saws for firewood and small trees.
  • $450 to $700: pro-grade gas saws (ECHO, Husqvarna) for heavy cutting and felling.
  • $700 and up: professional logging and arborist saws.
