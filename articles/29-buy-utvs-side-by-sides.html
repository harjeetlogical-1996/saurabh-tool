What to look for when buying UTVs and side-by-sides

Utvs and side-by-sides buying checklist

  • New vs used: new gives warranty, used saves money but needs inspection.
  • Sport vs utility: RZR/Maverick for sport, Ranger/Defender/UFORCE for work.
  • Seating: 2-seat vs 4-seat for passengers or cargo.
  • Warranty: factory warranty is a big plus on new machines.
  • Dealer support: confirm local service and parts availability.
  • Total cost: add freight, setup, tax, and accessories to the sticker price.

How much should you spend on UTVs and side-by-sides?

  • $2,000 to $8,000: used utility UTVs and older sport models.
  • $8,000 to $15,000: value new UTVs (CFMOTO UFORCE/ZFORCE).
  • $15,000 to $25,000: mainstream new Ranger, Defender, general-purpose UTVs.
  • $25,000 and up: premium sport RZR and Maverick performance models.
