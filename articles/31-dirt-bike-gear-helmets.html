What to look for when buying dirt bike gear and helmets

Dirt bike gear and helmets buying checklist

  • Helmet certification: look for DOT and ideally ECE or Snell ratings.
  • Fit: a helmet should be snug with no pressure points; try in person if possible.
  • Full coverage: helmet, goggles, gloves, boots, and body protection.
  • Brand authenticity: buy genuine brands from reputable sellers.
  • New helmets only: never buy a used helmet that may have been crashed.
  • Sizing charts: use each brand's chart, sizes vary between makers.

How much should you spend on dirt bike gear and helmets?

  • $100 to $300: entry helmet, goggles, and gloves to get riding.
  • $300 to $600: mid-range helmet plus boots and body armor.
  • $600 to $1,000: premium helmet and full protective kit.
  • $1,000 and up: top-tier helmet, neck brace, and race-level gear.
