What to look for when buying beginner dirt bikes

Beginner dirt bikes buying checklist

  • Engine size: 50cc to 110cc for kids, 125cc to 250cc for adult beginners.
  • Seat height: feet should reach the ground flat for confidence.
  • Start type: electric start is far easier for new riders than kick start.
  • Throttle limiter: lets you cap speed while learning.
  • Parts availability: name brands and own-brand sellers both make parts easy.
  • Warranty: even a 1-year warranty saves money on early issues.

How much should you spend on beginner dirt bikes?

  • $300 to $700: youth and kids beginner bikes (GoKarts USA, MotoBuys).
  • $700 to $1,500: entry-level adult value bikes (XProUSA, Tribal, Venom).
  • $1,500 to $3,500: quality name-brand beginner bikes (Cycle Trader, MX Locker).
  • $3,500 and up: new Honda, Yamaha, or KTM trail bikes.
