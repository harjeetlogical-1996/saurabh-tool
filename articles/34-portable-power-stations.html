What to look for when buying portable power stations

Portable power stations buying checklist

  • Capacity (Wh): match watt-hours to how much you need to run and for how long.
  • Output (W): ensure the continuous wattage covers your biggest device.
  • Battery type: LiFePO4 lasts far longer than older lithium cells.
  • Ports: confirm enough AC, USB-C, and 12V outputs for your gear.
  • Recharge speed: fast wall charging and solar input add flexibility.
  • Weight: bigger capacity means more weight, balance it to your use.

How much should you spend on portable power stations?

  • $200 to $400: small units for phones, laptops, and lights (Jackery 300).
  • $400 to $900: mid units for camping and short outages (Bluetti AC70).
  • $900 to $1,700: large units for home essentials (EcoFlow Delta, Bluetti Elite 200).
  • $1,700 and up: whole-home and RV-class units (Anker F3800).
