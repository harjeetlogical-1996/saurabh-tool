What to look for when buying emergency backup power

Emergency backup power buying checklist

  • Need: portable for essentials, whole-home for full coverage.
  • Capacity (kWh): size storage to how long you must run during outages.
  • Output (W): ensure it powers your biggest loads like AC or a well pump.
  • Battery vs fuel: batteries are quiet and clean; generators run indefinitely.
  • Expandability: modular systems let you grow capacity later.
  • Installation: permanent systems need certified electricians.

How much should you spend on emergency backup power?

  • $1,000 to $3,000: portable power stations for essentials.
  • $3,000 to $6,000: starter modular home battery systems.
  • $6,000 to $15,000: whole-home battery backup systems.
  • $15,000 and up: large installed battery and standby generator setups.
