What to look for in a leaf blower

Leaf blower buying checklist

  • CFM and MPH: CFM (air volume) moves piles, MPH (air speed) loosens stuck leaves. For most yards, look for 400-plus CFM.
  • Gas vs battery: battery blowers are quiet and low-maintenance for small to medium yards; gas backpacks clear large properties faster.
  • Handheld vs backpack: handheld is light and fine for driveways; backpack is more comfortable for big jobs.
  • Battery platform: stick to one brand (EGO, STIHL) so batteries are shared across tools.
  • Noise: battery blowers are much quieter, which matters in neighborhoods.
  • Warranty: buy from the brand or an authorized seller for coverage and parts.

How much should you spend on a leaf blower?

  • $99 to $200: handheld gas and battery blowers for small yards and driveways.
  • $200 to $350: premium battery blowers (EGO 670 CFM) for medium yards.
  • $350 to $600: gas backpack blowers (STIHL BR 800, Husqvarna 360BT) for large properties.
