What to look for before buying towing gear online

Before you click buy, run through this quick checklist. It is the same one we used to score the stores above.

Towing gear buying checklist

  • Fitment first: use a custom fit guide (etrailer) to match the hitch to your exact year, make, and model.
  • Hitch class: Class 1 to 2 for light loads and bikes, Class 3 to 4 for trailers and campers, Class 5 and 5th-wheel for the heaviest loads.
  • Weight ratings: check both gross trailer weight (GTW) and tongue weight, and never exceed your vehicle's tow rating.
  • Strap ratings: match the working load and break strength of straps and shackles to your load, with a safety margin.
  • Wiring: confirm you have the right connector (4-pin vs 7-pin) and a wiring harness for trailer lights and brakes.
  • Installation: decide DIY (etrailer videos) or professional (U-Haul) before you buy.

Red flags to avoid when buying towing gear online

Watch out for these

  • Buying a hitch without confirming exact vehicle fitment; the wrong class or fit is unsafe.
  • Tow straps or shackles with no stated working load or break strength.
  • Cheap hitches that do not list a weight rating or brand reputation.
  • Trailers sold without mention of freight delivery, title, or registration requirements.
  • Skipping the proper wiring harness, which leaves trailer lights and brakes non-functional.
