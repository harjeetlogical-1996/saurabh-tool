What to look for in an electric dirt bike

Electric dirt bike buying checklist

  • Voltage and power: 36V to 48V suits youth and casual riders; 60V to 72V (Sur-Ron, Talaria, E Ride Pro) is for serious trail performance.
  • Battery and range: check the amp-hours (Ah) and watt-hours; a swappable battery (E Ride Pro SR) extends ride time.
  • Street-legal: most electric dirt bikes are off-road only. Confirm your state's rules before riding on roads.
  • Warranty: buy from an authorized dealer (REV Rides) for a real 1 to 2 year warranty.
  • Weight and size: match the bike to the rider; performance bikes are heavier.
  • Parts and support: stores like Luna Cycle and REV Rides offer parts and upgrades you will want later.

How much should you spend on an electric dirt bike?

  • $300 to $700: electric kids bikes with speed limits (Best Buy, Razor).
  • $1,500 to $2,500: budget adult electric bikes (XProUSA DRAX, MotoBuys).
  • $3,000 to $5,000: trail performance bikes (Talaria Sting, E Ride Pro SS).
  • $5,000+: flagship models like the E Ride Pro SR with top speed and swappable batteries.
