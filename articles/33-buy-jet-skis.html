What to look for when buying jet skis and personal watercraft

Jet skis and personal watercraft buying checklist

  • New vs used: new gives warranty, used saves money but needs inspection.
  • Hours & history: on used PWC, check engine hours and service records.
  • Type: rec-lite for budget, luxury touring, or performance models.
  • Trailer: factor in a trailer if you do not already have one.
  • Warranty: factory warranty is a big plus on new machines.
  • Total cost: add freight, prep, registration, and safety gear.

How much should you spend on jet skis and personal watercraft?

  • $3,000 to $7,000: used rec-lite and older PWC models.
  • $7,000 to $11,000: new entry and rec-lite Sea-Doo and Yamaha.
  • $11,000 to $16,000: mid-range touring and family PWC.
  • $16,000 and up: performance and luxury models like GP and RXT.
