What to look for in a cheap dirt bike

Cheap dirt bike buying checklist

  • Engine size: 50cc to 110cc for kids and beginners, 125cc for teens and lighter adults. Do not overbuy on power.
  • Transmission: fully automatic is easiest to learn on; manual gives more control as you progress.
  • Warranty: many cheap bikes have only 90 days; XProUSA's 1-year frame warranty is a real plus.
  • Parts access: buy from a store like GoKarts USA that stocks spares, since budget bikes need maintenance.
  • Assembly: most arrive 85 to 95 percent assembled; budget an hour or a small shop fee.
  • Free shipping: on a cheap bike, shipping cost matters, so favor stores that include it.

How much should you spend on a cheap dirt bike?

  • $500 to $700: 50cc to 70cc youth and small pit bikes (MotoBuys, Q9).
  • $700 to $900: 110cc youth and entry 125cc bikes (Coolster, Tao Motor).
  • $900 to $1,000: better-equipped 125cc pit bikes (Apollo X9, X-PRO X9).
  • Plus $200 to $400: always budget for safety gear on top of the bike.
