What to look for in an electric scooter

Electric scooter buying checklist

  • Range: match it to your commute. 15 to 20 miles suits short trips; 40 to 50 miles (Segway Max G3, GoTrax G6) for longer rides.
  • Top speed: 15 to 20 mph is plenty for most; 28 mph (Max G3) for faster commutes, where legal.
  • Weight limit: check the max rider weight, especially for hill-climbing performance.
  • Portability: if you carry it on transit or upstairs, a lightweight folding model (Unagi) is best.
  • Brakes and tires: dual brakes and pneumatic tires give better stopping and a smoother ride.
  • Warranty: buy from the brand or an authorized seller for real warranty coverage.

How much should you spend on an electric scooter?

  • $180 to $350: budget commuter scooters (Segway C2, GoTrax GXL V2) for short city trips.
  • $350 to $700: mid-range scooters with better range and build (NIU KQi, GoTrax G6).
  • $700 to $1,000: long-range and feature-rich models (Segway Max G3).
  • $1,000 and up: premium performance and ultralight scooters (Apollo, Unagi).
