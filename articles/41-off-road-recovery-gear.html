What to look for when buying off-road recovery gear

Off-road recovery gear buying checklist

  • Rated capacity: match strap and shackle ratings to your vehicle's weight.
  • Kinetic vs static: kinetic ropes stretch to free stuck vehicles; static for towing.
  • Soft shackles: lighter and safer than steel for many recoveries.
  • Winch line: synthetic rope is safer and lighter than steel cable.
  • Traction boards: essential for sand, mud, and snow self-recovery.
  • Gloves & damper: always use recovery gloves and a line damper for safety.

How much should you spend on off-road recovery gear?

  • $50 to $150: basic straps, soft shackles, and gloves.
  • $150 to $400: kinetic ropes, traction boards, and quality shackles.
  • $400 to $900: full recovery kits and synthetic winch line.
  • $900 and up: premium winches and complete ARB-grade kits.
