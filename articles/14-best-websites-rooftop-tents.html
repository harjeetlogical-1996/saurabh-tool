What to look for in a rooftop tent

Rooftop tent buying checklist

  • Roof rack limits: check your rack's dynamic weight (driving) and static weight (parked, with people inside). This is the most important step.
  • Soft shell vs hard shell: soft-shell tents are cheaper and sleep more; hardshell tents open faster, are more aerodynamic, and last longer.
  • Capacity: a 3-person tent comfortably sleeps 2 adults and a child. Size up for comfort.
  • Weight: heavier tents need a stronger rack and two people to mount.
  • Materials: look for rip-stop polyester or canvas and a quality mattress and ladder.
  • Warranty: premium brands (Thule, Rhino) offer longer warranties; confirm coverage before buying.

How much should you spend on a rooftop tent?

  • $800 to $1,200: entry soft-shell tents (OVS TMBK 3, budget Amazon models) for getting started.
  • $2,000 to $3,300: aluminum hardshell tents (OVS XD Everest, Thule Tepui) that open faster and last longer.
  • $4,000+: premium hardshell tents and complete systems (Rhino Adventure Gear) for serious overlanders.
