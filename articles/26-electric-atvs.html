What to look for in an electric ATV

Electric ATV buying checklist

  • Battery range: check real-world range, not just the max claim. Match it to your ride length.
  • Charging time: some charge in hours, others overnight. Confirm the charger is included.
  • Power (voltage/hp): 36V to 48V for youth and light use, premium models like the Can-Am for full power.
  • Warranty: the battery is the priciest part, so a clear battery warranty matters most.
  • Use case: quiet utility for farms, sport for trails, or a youth quad for kids.
  • Weight limit: match the quad to the rider, especially for youth models.

How much should you spend on an electric ATV?

  • $300 to $1,500: youth and kids' electric quads (Amazon, Venom).
  • $1,500 to $3,000: affordable adult electric quads (Venom E-Grizzly).
  • $3,000 to $10,000: mid-range utility and specialty e-ATVs (Tractor Supply, Electric Ride Co).
  • $12,000 and up: premium new machines (Can-Am Outlander Electric, Polaris Kinetic).
