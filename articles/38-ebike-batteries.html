What to look for when buying ebike batteries

Ebike batteries buying checklist

  • Cell brand: insist on named cells like Samsung, Panasonic, or LG.
  • Voltage match: the pack must match your motor and controller voltage.
  • Connector & mount: confirm the plug and mounting fit your bike.
  • BMS: a quality Battery Management System is essential for safety.
  • Capacity (Ah): higher amp-hours mean more range.
  • Warranty: a 1-year warranty signals a quality pack.

How much should you spend on ebike batteries?

  • $200 to $350: smaller 36V/48V replacement packs.
  • $350 to $550: mid-capacity 48V/52V quality packs.
  • $550 to $800: high-capacity 52V/60V premium packs.
  • $800 and up: 72V and large-capacity custom builds.
