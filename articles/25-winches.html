What to look for in a winch

Winch buying checklist

  • Capacity: choose a winch rated for at least 1.5 times your vehicle's gross weight for safe recovery.
  • Vehicle match: ATVs need 2,500 to 3,500 lb, two-seat UTVs 3,500 to 4,500 lb, trucks 8,000 to 12,000-plus lb.
  • Rope type: synthetic rope is lighter and safer than steel cable; steel is cheaper and more abrasion-resistant.
  • Motor: series-wound motors pull harder, permanent-magnet motors are lighter and cheaper.
  • Mount: confirm you have or can buy a mounting plate or bumper for your vehicle.
  • Warranty: WARN and Smittybilt offer strong warranties; buy from an authorized seller.

How much should you spend on a winch?

  • $80 to $200: budget ATV/UTV winches (OpenRoad4WD, eBay used) for light recovery.
  • $200 to $450: quality value winches (Smittybilt XRC GEN2, Superwinch Terra 45).
  • $400 to $700: premium truck winches (WARN VR EVO 10) for serious recovery.
  • $700 and up: heavy-duty and high-capacity industrial winches.
