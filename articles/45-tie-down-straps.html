What to look for when buying tie-down straps and towing accessories

Tie-down straps and towing accessories buying checklist

  • Working load limit: pick straps rated above your cargo weight.
  • Type: ratchet for heavy loads, cam buckle for lighter, gentler tension.
  • End fittings: hooks, flat, or soft loops to match your anchor points.
  • Length: enough to wrap and tension without excess slack.
  • Wheel straps: use dedicated wheel straps for transporting vehicles.
  • Webbing condition: inspect for fraying and replace worn straps.

How much should you spend on tie-down straps and towing accessories?

  • $15 to $40: basic ratchet and cam strap sets.
  • $40 to $90: heavier-duty rated strap kits.
  • $90 to $200: wheel straps and transport-grade sets.
  • $200 and up: industrial cargo control and large kits.
