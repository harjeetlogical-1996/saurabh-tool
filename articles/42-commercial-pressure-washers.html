What to look for when buying commercial pressure washers

Commercial pressure washers buying checklist

  • Hot vs cold: hot water cuts grease and grime far better for many jobs.
  • Power source: electric for indoors, gas or diesel for mobile power.
  • PSI & GPM: balance pressure and flow for your cleaning tasks.
  • Duty cycle: commercial pumps and engines handle daily heavy use.
  • Portability: portable for job sites, stationary for fixed wash bays.
  • Service & parts: confirm local dealer support for a working machine.

How much should you spend on commercial pressure washers?

  • $1,000 to $2,500: entry commercial cold-water units.
  • $2,500 to $5,000: mid commercial hot or cold water machines.
  • $5,000 to $10,000: industrial hot-water and engine-driven units.
  • $10,000 and up: large stationary and diesel industrial systems.
