What to look for when buying trailers

Trailers buying checklist

  • Type: utility, enclosed cargo, car hauler, or specialty for your needs.
  • Capacity: match GVWR and payload to what you will haul.
  • Axles: single axle for light loads, tandem for heavy or highway use.
  • Towing match: confirm your vehicle and hitch can tow the loaded weight.
  • Title & registration: factor in local titling and plate requirements.
  • Warranty: longer warranties (like Renown's) protect your investment.

How much should you spend on trailers?

  • $1,000 to $2,500: small single-axle utility trailers.
  • $2,500 to $5,000: larger utility and small enclosed trailers.
  • $5,000 to $10,000: tandem-axle enclosed cargo trailers.
  • $10,000 and up: premium, custom, and large enclosed trailers.
