What to look for before buying a pressure washer online

Before you click buy, run through this quick checklist. It is the same one we used to score the stores above.

Pressure washer buying checklist

  • Electric vs gas: electric (1,300 to 2,300 PSI) is quiet and light for cars, patios, and furniture; gas (2,500 to 4,000+ PSI) is for driveways, decks, and heavy jobs.
  • PSI and GPM: PSI is cleaning pressure, GPM is water flow. Higher GPM rinses faster. Look at both, not just PSI.
  • Nozzles: quick-connect nozzle sets (0, 15, 25, 40 degree and soap) give you flexibility for different surfaces.
  • Pump quality: better pumps (triplex on higher-end models) last far longer than entry axial pumps.
  • Warranty: 1 to 3 years is common; commercial units often have longer pump warranties.
  • Hot vs cold water: only industrial machines (PowerWash.com) offer hot water, which is needed for grease and heavy grime.

Red flags to avoid when buying a pressure washer online

Watch out for these

  • Listings that quote a huge PSI but hide the GPM (water flow), which matters just as much.
  • No clear pump or engine warranty, the pump is the part that fails first.
  • Unknown brands far cheaper than Sun Joe or Simpson with no real reviews.
  • Commercial units sold without mention of freight delivery or assembly.
  • Missing or limited nozzle sets, which limit what surfaces you can safely clean.
