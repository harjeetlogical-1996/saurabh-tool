What to look for in a riding mower

Riding mower buying checklist

  • Lawn size: 1/2 to 1 acre suits a lawn tractor; 1 to 3 acres a larger tractor or zero-turn; 3+ acres a commercial zero-turn.
  • Deck size: a wider deck (42 to 60 inches) cuts faster but needs more storage and turning room.
  • Lawn tractor vs zero-turn: tractors are easier for beginners and slopes; zero-turns are faster and cut tighter around obstacles.
  • Robotic option: for lawns up to about an acre, a robotic mower (Lowe's, Husqvarna) mows hands-free.
  • Delivery: riding mowers ship by freight; confirm assembly, liftgate, and any setup fees.
  • Service network: buy a brand with local dealers for engine service and parts.

How much should you spend on a riding mower?

  • $1,500 to $2,500: entry lawn tractors for 1/2 to 1 acre (Cub Cadet XT1, Craftsman).
  • $2,500 to $4,000: larger tractors and entry zero-turns for 1 to 3 acres.
  • $4,000 to $8,000: premium and commercial zero-turns (Husqvarna, Bad Boy) for big properties.
  • $1,500 to $3,500: robotic mowers for hands-free cutting on lawns up to an acre.
