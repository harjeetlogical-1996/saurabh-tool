==================================================
  BACKGROUND MUSIC — yahan royalty-free tracks daalo
==================================================

Is folder me .mp3 ya .m4a music files daalo. Tool har reel ke liye
randomly ek track pick karega aur halki (15%) volume me lagayega.

>> ZAROORI: Sirf ROYALTY-FREE / copyright-free music use karo,
   warna Facebook copyright strike de dega.

Free copyright-free music kahan se milega:
-------------------------------------------
1. YouTube Audio Library (BEST, 100% free):
   https://studio.youtube.com  ->  left menu  ->  Audio Library
   "No copyright" wale tracks download karo.

2. Pixabay Music (free, no attribution):
   https://pixabay.com/music/

3. Free Music Archive:
   https://freemusicarchive.org/

Tip: alag-alag mood ke 4-5 tracks daalo:
   - calm / cinematic  (facts ke liye)
   - emotional / piano (quotes ke liye)
   - upbeat            (general)

File naam me mood likh do to behtar, jaise:
   calm_space.mp3
   emotional_piano.mp3
   cinematic_epic.mp3

Agar ye folder khaali hai, tool bina music ke reel bana dega (no error).
